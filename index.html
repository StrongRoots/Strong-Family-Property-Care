Questions, Answered

Can I really cancel anytime?

Yes. Give us 30 days notice and you're out — no fees, no retention calls. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

How does the annual plan work?

Pay for 10 months, get 12. Essential $550/yr, Complete $1,150/yr, Premium $1,550/yr. Same 30-day cancellation — we refund the unused months.

Do I need to be home for visits?

No. Almost everything we do is exterior. We text you before each visit so you know we're coming, and you come home to it done.

Who provides the Christmas lights?

You buy the lights once (we'll recommend the right ones for your roofline), and we handle install, takedown, and storage guidance every year after.

What if it's snowy or icy when my Christmas lights are due?

Christmas light installs are scheduled through December 10th. If you booked before December 10th, we'll get to you as quickly as possible. If snow or ice makes roof work unsafe, we install ground decorations only until the roof is clear. If weather and roof conditions keep us from getting your home done at all, you pick one other service from your plan at no extra cost.

What if I need something not in my plan?

Just text us. Plan members get priority scheduling and 10% off any extra one-time work.

How does snow removal work on Premium?

When it snows, we clear your driveway. Premium members are first on the route after every storm — no calling, no waiting list.
